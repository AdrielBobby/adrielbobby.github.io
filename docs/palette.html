Rules

  • One site colour. Patina green belongs to the site: nav, links, focus rings, the logo tile. No project uses it.
  • Every accent has two variants. The lighter one is for dark and green surfaces, the darker one for light surfaces. Never use one hex for both.
  • Accents carry meaning. They identify a branch or project (Security, Hardware, Backend, Local AI, Dashboards). They are not decoration.
  • No more than two strong accents in one view, from the first calibration. The grey and green base does the rest.
  • Text stays dark on green. Near-black on the green page; pale grey on dark; dark grey on light.
