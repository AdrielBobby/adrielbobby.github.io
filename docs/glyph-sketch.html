SKILL TREE / CUSTOM GLYPHS

Glyph sketch

Drawn for the 28 skills that have no logo in the Simple Icons set. They are generic symbols for what each tool does, not copies of any tool's own logo, so they carry no trademark risk. The top row shows five real logos next to five glyphs so you can judge whether they sit together.

  • Grid: 24 by 24, drawn to fill about 18 units, so every glyph has the same visual size.
  • Stroke: 2 units, round caps and joins, no fills. The real logos are solid, so glyphs use a heavier stroke to read at a similar weight.
  • Concept: one idea per glyph. Where two tools do similar jobs (the three scanners, the two stego tools) they share a frame and differ by one inner symbol.
  • Colour: tinted in the branch colour, never in brand colours.
